Sustainability at Mesh Bioplastics

Designing materials and manufacturing processes that support a more sustainable, circular future — without compromising performance.

Why sustainability matters

In healthcare and advanced manufacturing, sustainability isn't optional — it directly impacts safety, reliability, cost, and long-term scalability.

Regulatory pressure

Healthcare materials must increasingly meet environmental standards without compromising performance or compliance.

Material efficiency

Reducing waste and variability improves yield, consistency, and long-term manufacturing economics.

Resilient supply chains

Circular and bio-based materials reduce exposure to volatile raw material markets and supply disruptions.

Our Sustainability Approach

Sustainability is embedded into how we design materials, run manufacturing, and work with partners.

♻️

Circular Design

We design materials with their full lifecycle in mind — supporting reuse, recycling, and responsible end-of-life where applicable.

⚙️

Responsible Manufacturing

Our manufacturing processes are optimised to reduce waste, energy use, and material variability — improving consistency and efficiency.

🏥

Sustainable Healthcare Innovation

We support healthcare innovation by delivering materials that meet strict performance and regulatory needs while advancing sustainability.

Aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals

We focus on outcomes that support health, innovation, responsible production, climate action, and partnerships.

Our Commitment

Across products and partnerships, we are committed to continuously improving our sustainability performance and transparency as we scale.
