Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Mesh collects, uses, and protects personal information in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who We Are

Mesh is responsible for the processing of personal data collected through this website. If you have any questions regarding this policy, you may contact us via our contact page.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Industry or company information (if provided)
  • Message content submitted through our contact form
  • Technical data such as IP address and browser type (via analytics tools)

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Communicate regarding partnerships or investor enquiries
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Support business development activities

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent (where you submit information voluntarily)
  • Legitimate interest (website analytics and service improvement)
  • Contractual necessity (where applicable)

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or to comply with legal obligations.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by trusted third-party providers such as website hosting or analytics services, where appropriate safeguards are in place.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission

8. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please contact us via our Contact page.
